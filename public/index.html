Essential Crypto Guides

Clear steps for the moments that matter: protect your assets, avoid common traps, and recover with care.

01 / SECURITYWallet basicsLost your recovery phrase? What to do nextRead the guide ↓

If you still control the wallet: create a new wallet with a fresh recovery phrase, verify the backup offline, then transfer assets to its new address. Keep the phrase offline in a private place; never type it into a website or share it with support.

If the phrase is lost and you are locked out: no legitimate service can recreate it. Beware anyone promising recovery for an upfront fee, and never approve remote access for them.

02 / TRANSACTIONSSend & receiveBefore you send: prevent an irreversible transferRead the guide ↓

Confirm the asset, exact network, and full destination address. Check the address on the receiving platform itself; a familiar token name or logo is not proof. Send a small test amount first when practical, then wait for confirmation before sending the rest.

Crypto transfers are often irreversible. If an address or network is wrong, contact the receiving service promptly, but recovery is not guaranteed.

03 / SCAM DEFENCEStay alertSpot wallet-draining scams and fake supportRead the guide ↓

Stop if a message pressures you to act, offers guaranteed returns, or asks for your recovery phrase, private key, or a screen-share. Verify support through the app or website you opened yourself; avoid links in a message or search ad.

Review token approvals before signing. Disconnecting a site does not revoke existing spending approvals; use trusted approval tools for that network if you need to revoke one.

04 / NETWORKSTroubleshootingSent crypto on the wrong network? Start hereRead the guide ↓

Find the transaction hash in your wallet and check it on the block explorer for the network you used. Confirm that the receiving service supports that asset on that network before taking action.

If funds went to an exchange deposit address, contact its official support with the transaction hash, asset, and network. Never share your recovery phrase. Recovery depends on the recipient and may not be possible.

05 / ASSET RISKStablecoinsStablecoins: understand the risks behind the pegRead the guide ↓

Stable describes a target price, not a guarantee. A stablecoin can lose its peg, restrict transfers, or become hard to redeem. Check the issuer, redemption terms, reserve disclosures, and exact token contract on the relevant network.

Avoid treating any single stablecoin as cash or a guaranteed store of value. Consider whether you can afford delays or losses before holding it.

06 / ACCOUNT SAFETYProtect accessSecure your exchange account in five minutesRead the guide ↓

Use a unique password stored in a password manager, enable passkeys or authenticator-based two-factor authentication, and turn on withdrawal allowlisting and login alerts if available. Secure the email account linked to your exchange as well.

Keep only the amount you need on a custodial platform, and verify its official domain before signing in. Never share one-time codes with someone who contacts you.
